How one DM becomes an order

Customer writes

On Messenger, Instagram or this page. Arabic, English or Franco: "3ayez 2 latte".

Gemini reads

The AI only understands the intent and the items. It never decides a price.

Sheet decides

Prices, totals, hours and limits come from code and the Menu sheet, so nothing is invented.

A Telegram alert wakes the kitchen; one form message tells the customer it is ready.
